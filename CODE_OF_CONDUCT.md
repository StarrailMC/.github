# Code of Conduct

Voxaris Forge is a small team, and we want it to be a welcoming place to contribute — whether you're here for one PR or sticking around long term.

## Expectations

- Be respectful. Disagree on ideas, not people.
- Be constructive. Criticism is welcome; put-downs aren't.
- Be patient. This is a small team working in spare time — response times will vary.
- No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind, in repos or in the Discord.

## Reporting an Issue

If someone's behavior in a repo, issue, PR, or the Discord makes you uncomfortable, reach out to a team member directly on [Discord](https://discord.gg/CFUYP246zU). Reports will be handled discreetly.

## Enforcement

Team leads may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict access for anyone who violates this code of conduct.
